# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not the person.

## What that means here

**Good.** Asking why something is done a certain way. Disagreeing with a design and saying what
you would do instead. Pointing out a bug bluntly. Saying "I do not know". Changing your mind.

**Not good.** Personal attacks, insults, or dismissing someone for being new. Harassment of any
kind, public or private. Sexual language or imagery. Publishing anyone's private information.
Sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainers privately, through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/iofhouras/AutoVM/security/advisories/new) if you would
rather it stayed off the public record. Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit,
remove or reject contributions that do not follow this document, and may block accounts for
repeated or serious behaviour.
